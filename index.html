The Artist

Hello, I'm Shri

I've always been drawn to the energy of wildflowers — the way they grow without permission, in every colour at once. My acrylic paintings try to capture that feeling: abundant, unapologetic, alive.

I work with heavy body acrylics and professional gel medium to build up thick, raised textures. Every petal is applied with a palette knife or my fingertips — you can feel the brushwork when you run your hand across the surface.

Based in Sydney, I paint in my home studio and ship worldwide. Commissions are very welcome — tell me your colours and your space, and I'll bring it to life.

— With love & colour
